# User Guide

Every feature of Notes, section by section. Tap a section to open it; use the search box above to find a feature by name. **Show me** under a section takes you to the matching screen.

## Getting started {#start}
Your notes belong to your account and **sync instantly** between phone, tablet, computer and the web. Everything works offline too; changes sync by themselves when you are back online.
- Sign in with your Google account, or start right away without signing in.
- **Attachments (images, PDFs, slides, audio) live in your Google Drive:** if you signed in with Google, go to Settings → Sync → **Keep attachments in your Google Drive → Connect** (or tap the "waiting for Google Drive" strip on the home screen). Attachments use your own Drive storage, sit in a hidden app folder and download to your other devices. Attachments of notes you share with others go to the app's cloud so everyone can see them.
- **Without an account (guest), your notes stay on this device only:** they cannot be opened elsewhere and are gone if you uninstall the app. Built-in AI, contacts and sharing need a Google account (they show a lock and explain why when tapped); you can still use AI by adding your own Gemini key in Settings. Link a Google account any time — your notes carry over.
- Want a note to stay **on this device only**? Note menu (⋮) → **Stop syncing**. That note and its attachments never go to the cloud.
- Videos are always stored only on the device that recorded them (never uploaded); YouTube videos are links, so they play on every device.
- Made a mistake? **Undo** (↶) and **Redo** on the note screen bring back every change.

## Home screen {#home}
Your notes appear as cards grouped by **TODAY / THIS WEEK / EARLIER**; pinned notes stay on top.
- **In list view**, swipe a row **right** to pin it and **left** to archive it (undoable). **In grid view**, swiping the screen sideways moves between folders (or filters) — Settings → Notes and folders → **Swipe sideways on home**.
- **Long-press a card:** multi-select — move, archive, duplicate or delete several notes at once.
- **Search:** type in the box at the top. Tapping the box reveals the ✨ **Meaning** switch on its right: off, it finds words; on, it understands what you mean ("car expenses" also finds the "tyre change" note).
- **Avatar at the top right:** your account. The number on it counts pending friend requests; tap it for **Contacts · Premium · Account settings**.
- **Inbox** (envelope icon): in the folder strip, right next to **All**. Copies sent to you and notes shared with you are in **one list**; a small tag on each item tells them apart (**Received** = a copy waiting to be accepted, **Shared** = a shared note). Tapping the chip filters the home screen like any folder; switching to another folder or All, or pressing back, clears it. A red badge appears on the chip's corner when something new arrives.
- **Filter:** **View** (grid or list; images on cards, attachments on cards, shared notes and **shared notes below mine** as switches), **Type** (text, list, numbered), **Status** (pinned, starred, with alarm, locked, no folder or tag), tag and **Sort** (recently updated by default — date groups show in this order; title, most worked on; **Reorder manually** to drag cards into place).
- **Folder strip:** switch folders by tapping or by **swiping sideways**. The number of notes is written small at the top-right corner of each folder icon; empty folders are faded and last. The **folders icon** (All folders) at the right of the strip opens every folder; reorder them there.
- **Quick note row** (off by default): turn it on in Settings → **Modules & quick note** → Quick note row, then type into "New note…" at the top of the list and save — without opening a note.
- **Home screen background:** ⋮ → Home screen background for an image or pattern.
- **Media on cards:** a pinned or single image/video shows as a cover at the top of the card; videos carry ▶, YouTube the red button. A bubbles note gets its document as the cover at the top of the card: the first page, the **PDF WITH BUBBLES** badge top left, the file name and bubble count at the bottom; voice recordings and other documents sit as rows at the bottom of the card.
- The ⋮ at the top right opens a sheet: **AI, Reminders, Archive, Bin, Settings** on top (on wide screens these live in the rail on the left and are not repeated in ⋮); below them **Home screen** (background, hidden folders) and **App** (personalise, this guide).
- **A card's ⋮ / long-press menu:** pin, star, move to folder, share, select on top; then **Organize** (colour — optionally with a border highlight), **Copy** (copy, duplicate, save as template), **Tools** (listen, statistics), **Protection and storage** (archive, lock, sync); Delete at the bottom.

## Creating notes {#create}
The **+** button at the bottom right opens a short panel, read from your thumb upwards:
- **At the bottom, right above the button:** large **Text note · List · Ordered list** buttons.
- Going up, the groups use the same names and order as **Add** inside a note: **Pen** (**Write on page (pen)**: write directly on the note page; **Separate drawing (image)**: draw on a separate canvas, added to the note as an image), **Camera** (photo, video, scan, QR), **Files and links** (**Add file**: one picker for images, PDF, slides, video or audio; images and videos also offer "Transcribe"; plus **YouTube video**) and **Templates** (from template, shopping list, **notebook**). A note started from a video opens with the video pinned on top and the note below.
- **The home screen has one microphone:** a tap starts **Dictate** (your words are typed into a new note, no quota); a **long-press starts an AI voice note** (AI builds the note — title, items and reminders).
A note always opens classic; when you add a file, the **Where should the attachment go?** tip lets you pick the view (see **Bubbles ↔ classic**). Files from the share menu open with the same tip. On the web you can drop a file on the page or paste an image: it goes into the open note, otherwise into a new note.

## Editing a note {#editor}
Bottom bar, left to right:
- **Aa — Format:** bold, italic, underline, text colour, highlight, heading / subheading; the note's font, size and transparency. Page colour, border highlight and background image live in the **palette** on the top bar (one place). Applies to the selection, or to the word you type next.
  - The panel's top row is the **scope**: **Cursor · Word · Line · All**. Pick "Line", put the cursor on a line, then B / colour / size **+ −** — the whole line changes, no manual selection. The choice is remembered for the session. When you select text yourself this row hides — the selection is the scope; **All** applies every format to the whole field.
  - On an item the panel has a **Line below** row: solid, dashed, dotted (tap the same one to remove) and a settings button.
- **+ — Add:** the groups of the + panel, with the same names: **Write** (text block, list, embed attachment in selection), **Pen** (write on page, separate drawing), **Camera** (take photo, record video, scan document, QR), **Files and links** (**Add file** — one picker: image, PDF, slides, video or audio —, image, YouTube video) and **Sound** (voice recording). Long-press an icon to see what it does.
- **🔍 — Search in note:** matches are highlighted as you type.
- **Share.**
- The coloured capsule on the right: **✦ AI** (with **AI voice note** at the top of its sheet; see the AI section), **Audio recording** and the **Dictate** microphone. Hide them from Personalise.
Top bar: **undo / redo** (always side by side), palette, pin and the ⋮ menu. The ⋮ menu's top row has star, move to folder, version history and listen; below are the same groups as the card menu: **Organize** (**Notebook view** and paper, convert to list/text, two columns, sort items, images to attachments, clear content), **Copy** (duplicate, save as template, **collect into notebook**), **Tools** (add to home screen, statistics, **calculator**), **Protection and storage** (archive, lock, sync). **Search** and **Share** also appear in this menu when they don't fit the bottom bar. A thin bottom row holds **Customize screen** and **Settings**. The bottom bar keeps the same order for every note type: Format · Add · Search · Share · Attachments, with the type-specific button (☑ or Select) always last. Copying to the clipboard, sending to a friend and exporting live in the **Share** sheet.
- In lists, long-press an item and drag to reorder; use "Select" to copy/delete several items. **Copy and check** in the selection bar copies the selected items and checks them in one tap.
- The **chip** next to the title (tag · alarm · person icons): **New page**, alarm, location reminder (Android), **Collaborators**, folder, tag and tag/title suggestions. The chip's person icon shows how many collaborators there are.
- The item you're editing shows a small **×** delete button on the right (the line width doesn't change); the item menu (long-press its left area) and the scribble gesture delete too.

## Item gestures {#gestures}
Draw over an item:
- **Strike through** (horizontal line) or a **✓** → the item is checked (or unchecked). Finger or pen.
- **Scribble** (zigzag) → the item is deleted; **Undo** brings it back. Pen only.
- **Circle** → the item's spelling is fixed (AI; spelling and punctuation only). Pen only.
- Scribble and circle are off for fingers so scrolling can't trigger them; turn them on in **Settings → Appearance → Delete and fix gestures with a finger too**.
- Nothing to turn on: a stroke that starts over an item is a gesture. Scrolling, long-presses and text selection don't count.
- A pen (S Pen etc.) writes on the page instead of making gestures. With **Detect pen automatically** off, gestures work with the pen too.
- Turn it off in **Settings → Appearance → Item gestures**.
- The first time you open a list note, a short tip appears once.

## Note pages {#pages}
A note can have several **pages**; each page has its own text and images.
1. Tap the **chip** next to the title → **New page**.
2. A **‹ Page 2 / 3 ›** bar appears under the title; use the arrows to move between pages.
3. The bar's **new page** button adds a page after the current one; **delete** removes the current page.
- Images of a deleted page are not lost — they move to the neighbouring page; the text comes back with **Undo**.
- Sharing and export include all pages in order.

## Images {#media}
Images **float freely** on the note's page; text flows around them.
- **Move:** press and hold an image and drag without lifting your finger. Edges snap to the page and other images, with alignment guides.
- **Resize:** tap the image (it gets selected), drag the bottom-right handle or use the slider below.
- **Preview:** double-tap a selected image or tap its corner handle.
- **Full screen:** Share and Copy attachment in the top bar; crop and rotate in ⋮; write on it with the pen, recognise its text (OCR).
- **View (Pinned / Floating / Bubbles):** on the top row of the selected image's panel. **Pinned** puts it on top (the note scrolls below), **Floating** keeps it on the page, **Bubbles** turns the image into a one-page document with bubbles, highlights and pen (the image's drawings come along). Back to Floating, the image returns to its place; switching to Bubbles again brings back the same document with its bubbles.
- **⋮ menu:** extract text, copy, select images (equalise sizes, fit 2/3 per row), move to attachments.
- An image can become the note's **background image** (palette on the top bar).

## Video and YouTube {#video}
- **+ Add → Record video** records with the camera; an existing video comes in through **+ Add → Add file**. The video sits on the page like an image and **plays in place**.
- **+ Add → YouTube video:** paste the link (a YouTube link on the clipboard is filled in for you). Thumbnail and title come automatically.
- Tap a YouTube box (to select it) and the **video's title** appears over the thumbnail.
- The red **▶** plays the video **inside the box**; the **×** at its corner closes the player so the box can be moved again. In a small box YouTube's own controls shrink with the box.
- **Copy link:** from the video's ⋮ menu, the 🔗 button on the pinned pane's bar, or the full-screen player. ⋮ → **Open in YouTube** opens it in the YouTube app.
- On the web version, YouTube also plays right inside the page.
- **Picture-in-picture:** tap ⧉ in the full-screen player, or go to the home screen while the video plays — it continues in a small window.
- **Make a note from the video:** from the video's ⋮ menu; AI watches and listens and writes a detailed note (for videos on the device).
- Share from the YouTube app → **Notes** and the video arrives as a playable attachment.
- **Timestamps (like YouTube):** when the note has a video, times such as **1:23**, **12:05** or **1:02:33** in the text are drawn in blue; tap one and the video jumps there (or starts there if it isn't playing). Tapping a timestamp doesn't open the keyboard — tap next to it to type.
- **Control bar:** once a video has played, its box shows **⏪ 10s · ▶/⏸ · 10s ⏩** at the bottom, plus position/length and **speed** (1× → 1.25× → 1.5× → 2× → 0.5× → 0.75×) in a wide box. A YouTube video playing on the page has its controls right below the box; a pinned video has them on the pane's bar.
- The full-screen player also has **speed** and **loop** buttons.
- **Note at this moment:** while the video plays, the **⏱ Note** button at its corner (on the bar of a pinned video, in the top bar full screen) writes the current timestamp and leaves you ready to type: a new item in a list note, or the timestamp on the caret's line (below it if the line has text) in a text note.

## Media on top, note below {#pin}
To take notes while watching a video or looking at an image:
1. Press and hold the image/video and drag it **upwards** (pushing it past the title is enough). only while dragging, a **"Release: pin to top"** strip appears at the top of the screen; it fills in when your finger gets there.
2. Release: the media stays fixed above the note, and the note scrolls on its own below. YouTube plays in place here too.
- Drag the **bar** between them to resize the pane. The bar has **Full screen** on the left and the **view switch** (Pinned / Floating / Bubbles) on the right; for videos also **⏪ 10s · ▶/⏸ · 10s ⏩**, speed and **⏱ Note**; the thin line along the bar's top edge is the video's progress.
- **Bubbles stay visible when pinned:** a PDF (or image) pinned from bubbles view keeps its bubbles; tap one to read it. The 💬 on the PDF box's bar (the button on the pane's bar for an image) **hides/shows** them.
- **PDFs can be pinned too:** the document stays on top, pages turn inside the box, and you write below.
- Pulling the bar all the way up, or holding the media and dragging it down, also puts it back. It returns to its old place and size; pin it again and the pane opens at its previous height.
- The same is available with the **Pinned** button on the selected attachment's panel.

## Embedding in text {#embed}
You can link a **PDF, image, video, YouTube video** or any attachment of the note to a word or sentence.
1. Select the word → **Embed** in the selection menu (or the same option under + Add).
2. The sheet shows the **attachments in this note** with thumbnails at the top; for a new one tap **PDF, Image, Video or YouTube** below.
3. The word becomes a rounded **chip** in the colour of the attachment's type, with the type's badge in front (PDF red, image teal, video purple, YouTube magenta).
- List items work the same: **Embed** in the selection menu, and **Preview / Edit / Remove embed** when the cursor is on a tag.
- **Tap the chip** (its last letter too) to open a preview card: videos and YouTube play in the card, PDF pages turn in the card. The card has **Full screen** and **Edit**, with **Replace** and **Remove embed** below.
- Removing an embed keeps the text and only unlinks it.
- It also works the other way round: pick the attachment first (link button on the attachment strip), then select the text and tap **Link here**. Or tap **✍ (circle)** on the strip and **circle** the text with your finger.
- The **View** switch on the preview card makes the attachment Pinned, Floating, Bubbles or Attachment; **Remove embed** unlinks the word.
- When an attachment is deleted, the chips of words linked to it go away too (the text stays).

## PDFs and slides {#documents}
In **Bubbles** mode the document is the note itself: the page on top, that page's note below.
- **Bubbles:** **press and hold** the spot on the page the note belongs to — a bubble drops there and the writing card opens. Or press **Bubble** in the bottom bar, then tap the page. The bubble's pointed corner marks that spot; press **Done** when you've written it.
- Tap a bubble to read it in place; **Edit** switches to writing. The writing card's bottom row: **colour** · **mark** (star or urgent — urgent blinks) · **Aa** (size − / +; bold, italic, alignment and fill on plain text; arrow on highlights) · delete. Long-press the bubble itself and drag to move it.
- **Get bubble text out:** **Copy** on the reading card; **Copy all** and **Add to note** (each bubble into its own page's page note) in the 💬 list; with the **Bubbles** chip on in the Share sheet they go into the text as "Page 3 — text" lines.
- **Size of all bubbles:** − / + on the card opened by the Bubble button (applies to every document on this device).
- **Show / hide:** the 👁 button in the bottom bar hides the document's bubbles and highlights without deleting them. They reappear when you drop a new bubble or go to one from the list.
- The 💬 button with the count lists and searches all bubbles. Page note, all page notes, edit pages and full screen live in the ⋮ menu on phones.
- **Highlight:** **double-tap** text (a word is selected; keep the finger down on the second tap and drag to extend), then highlight it; copy it or ask AI about it. A scanned page's text is recognised once and kept — no waiting next time.
- **Pen:** write on the page with the pen in the bottom bar; one finger draws, two fingers scroll. The pen tray is the same as on the handwriting screen (tap the selected pen again for colour/width); the thin row above it has undo, redo, pen only and settings.
- **Edit pages:** delete, rotate, reorder — page notes move with their pages.
- **Bookmarks** and page jumps; double-tap to zoom.
- **Chat with the PDF:** ask "is there a penalty clause?" or "what's the deadline?" (**This document** in the chat screen).
- In a classic note the PDF sits on the page as a small box; pages turn inside the box.
- **Switching views (Pinned / Floating / Bubbles):** every attachment has the same switch next to it — on the top row of the panel when you tap the PDF on the page, on the bar when it's pinned, and right of the title in bubbles view. ⋮ → *Switch to bubbles/classic view* works too. Switching deletes nothing: bubbles, highlights, drawings and page notes, as well as the PDF's position and size on the page, are kept and come back as they were.
- When a new note asks for the mode, **your last choice** is marked.
- In another app choose **Open with → Notes** for a PDF: you're asked whether to make a new note or add it to an existing one.

## Scan, OCR and QR {#scan}
- **Scan a document:** the camera finds the edges and flattens the page; adjust corners and filters on the review screen. The result is a **searchable PDF** or images.
- **Recognise text in an image:** in the image's full screen or ⋮ → Extract text; tap recognised lines to select them.
- **Scan a QR code:** from the camera or a gallery photo; the content is written into the note.

## Voice and dictation {#voice}
Four jobs, four names:
- **Dictate** (plain microphone in a note; tapping the home microphone): text appears at the cursor as you speak — uses no quota.
- **AI voice note** (top of the ✦ AI sheet; long-pressing the home microphone; top of the AI screen): say "dentist tomorrow at 9" and it creates the note, list and reminder itself.
- **Audio recording** (wave button in a note, or + Add → Voice recording): play it from the attachment strip, transcribe it with AI. An MP3/M4A/WAV file from your phone comes in through **+ Add → Add file**.
- **Listen** (top row of the note ⋮ menu): reads the note aloud; voice, speed and how the dictate microphone works are in Settings → **Listening & dictation**.

## Transcripts {#transcript}
- Tap the **subtitles icon** on an audio, video (YouTube too) or image tile in the attachment strip (or ⋮ → **Transcribe** on a picture/video on the page). Speech is written out with timestamps; text in images is read on the device (no internet).
- The result is saved in the note as a **text attachment (.txt)**: a paper-coloured "TXT" tile in the strip that opens for reading. No AI points are spent again; the icon turns solid.
- While reading, select text → **Add selection to note** or **New note**. With nothing selected the bottom button becomes **Add all to note**; timestamps enter the note as "1:23" and seek the video there.
- ⋮ menu: **Edit** (fix a misheard word), **Re-transcribe with AI** / on images **Read with AI (handwriting)**, hide timestamps, delete the transcript. The top buttons copy it or **share it as a .txt file**.
- **Transcribe a file** (+ menu or + Add): pick audio, video or an image; the file goes into the note and its text is written straight into the note body.
- Large recordings: the built-in AI accepts up to 15 MB; add your own Gemini key in Settings to transcribe long ones. On Android only the AUDIO track of a video is sent (much smaller).
- Transcripts are searchable: a word spoken in a recording finds the note that holds it.
- **Listen:** from the note menu, listen to the note (or a PDF/Word document via AI → Read a document); voice and speed in Settings → Listening & dictation.

## AI {#ai}
The **✦** button on the note screen:
- **Summarise note** (short / detailed), **Analyse** (main points and to-dos), **Fix up the note** (simple / balanced / detailed), **Improve the note** (formal, shorter, bullet or numbered list), **Translate**.
- **Chat:** one screen; the chips on top choose what you talk to — **All notes**, **This note** (its text and attachments) or **This document** — and, for a note, **Q&A** or **Brainstorm** (the AI drafts an outline, add an idea to the note with one tap).
- For attachments: summarise/ask a PDF, text from an image, transcribe audio.
The **AI** screen (⋮, or the rail on the left on wide screens):
- **AI voice note:** at the top; speak and it builds the note.
- **Note assistant:** ask about all your notes ("what's missing from my shopping list?"); while empty it offers ready-made suggestion buttons.
- **Brainstorm with AI:** it drafts an outline first, then you develop it together.
- **On your notes:** summarise the week, tag the untagged, suggest titles, clean up duplicates (duplicates use no AI). A box with nothing to do is faded and says **Nothing to do right now**.
- **Read a document:** listen to a PDF/Word/slides file.
- **Daily briefing:** reminders and recent notes at a glance; can also arrive as a morning notification.
- The **All notes** chat finds the notes that fit your question; the note chips under the answer open the notes it used.
- **Today's cards:** choose **Make cards** in a note's ⋮ menu and AI turns the note into question–answer cards; spaced repetition (Again · Hard · Good · Easy) brings each card back here on its day.
- Your daily points renew 24 hours after first use (Settings shows the time); with your own Gemini API key AI works without limits. The settings button at the top right (**AI settings**) opens the AI area of Settings.

## Organising: folders, tags, archive {#organize}
- **Folders:** create, colour and reorder them; move a note with ⋮ → Move to folder. A **hidden** folder's notes don't appear on the home screen; reveal them with ⋮ → Hidden folders and your fingerprint/face.
- **Tags:** add from the chip next to the title; long-press a tag in the filter sheet to rename or delete it.
- **Pin:** keep important notes on top and in the Pinned filter. **Star** is a separate mark (Starred filter).
- **Lock:** lock a note; choose how it opens in Settings → **Note lock** (fingerprint, password or pattern; password or pattern on the web). Its content never leaks into search, widgets or AI. A lock is not encryption: it only stops this app from showing the note.
- **Archive** and **Bin:** from the ⋮ menu; restore from the bin.
- **Note links:** type `[[` while writing, or select text and choose **Link to note**; the chosen note's name becomes a link that opens it (the link survives renames). A **N backlinks** chip under the linked note's title lists the notes pointing to it. Note ⋮ → **Related notes** suggests similar notes; **Link** adds one in a tap.
- **Templates:** + → From template; preview ready-made templates and pick items; turn your own note into one with ⋮ → **Save as template**, and change it in the template editor.
- **Notebook:** + → Templates → Notebook, or ⋮ → **Notebook view** in any note: lined/grid/dotted paper and pages. Collect another note's attachments into a notebook with ⋮ → **Collect into notebook**.

## Reminders {#reminders}
- Use **chip → Remind me** next to the title to pick a date and time; you get a notification when it's due.
- **Location reminder:** the note arrives as a notification when you arrive at or leave a place (location permission must be "always").
- **Repeat:** after the time, pick every day, every weekday, weekly (choose days), monthly or yearly; saying "every monday at 9" works too. **Done** on the notification moves it to the next time.
- All reminders are on the ⋮ → **Reminders** screen.
- The **Today** widget shows today's reminders with their times on your home screen.

## Sharing and working together {#share}
The **Share** button on the note screen: a preview of the text at the top with content chips (title, completed items, **bubbles**, tags, **video links** — your choice is remembered next time). Below are two sections; press and hold a button to see what it sends. **SEND** (to any app):
- **Share / Copy:** send the text to any app or copy it.
- **PDF:** send the note as a PDF in one tap (images at the end).
- **Image:** send the note as pictures (PNG pages) — previews nicely in chats.
- **Files:** send the note's photos, PDFs and videos as files.
- **Custom PDF / Word…** (title, image position/size) and **PDF with bubbles** (bubbles and drawings printed into the PDF).

**TO A NOT USER:** **Send to a friend** (an independent copy lands in their Inbox), **Edit together** (shared note or the note's folder) and **Note package** (one file with attachments, pages, drawings and bubbles; the other person opens it exactly the same in Notes).

An attachment you've opened (image, PDF, slides, video, YouTube) can be sent straight from **Share** in the full-screen viewer's top bar, or copied into another note with **Copy attachment**.
Working together:
- **Contacts:** add friends from the avatar at the top right → Contacts.
- **Send to a friend** (in the Share sheet): an independent copy of the note lands in your friend's **Inbox** on the home screen; once accepted it becomes their own note.
- **Shared note:** **chip** next to the title → **Collaborators**; you both edit the same note at the same time.
- **Folder sharing:** with read / write permission; a shared folder has its own chat.
From other apps: choose **Share → Notes** anywhere (text, link, image, PDF, slides, video). You choose whether to make a new note or add to an existing one.

## Widgets and shortcuts {#widgets}
Long-press your home screen → **Widgets → Notes**:
- **Notes:** recent notes and a quick voice note.
- **Quick Note:** start writing with one tap.
- **Quick Actions:** new note, list, numbered list, voice note.
- **Voice Note:** speak and let AI create the note.
- **Today:** today's reminders.
Long-press the app icon for the **Voice note** shortcut. On a computer, **Keyboard shortcuts** (Ctrl+/) in the side bar lists every shortcut.

## Settings {#settings}
Find any setting by name with the search box at the top of Settings ("briefing", "microphone").
At the top are two cards: **User guide** and **Personalise** (which buttons and areas show; **edit mode** starts from there too). Below are the areas; a closed area's summary line tells you what is selected inside.
- **Contacts:** friends, incoming notes, discoverability, shared-note notifications.
- **AI:** model, your own API key, daily briefing, auto tag/title, keeping checked items untouched.
- **Appearance:** sample text, theme (system, Sage, Porcelain, Clay, dark), font and size, **Language** (Turkish, English or the system language), long items, item gestures, **Shown on cards** (with a live sample card: counter, tag, date, image, attachments, folder, paper…), dark theme schedule.
- **Modules & quick note:** turn the AI, voice, video, scan, drawing, templates… modules on or off; the quick note row on the home screen; **Show tips again**.
- **Notes and folders:** sideways swipe, folder order, sorting, grid/list and columns (also by pinching on the home screen), shared notes on the home screen, notes from content (Bubbles/Classic), searching inside attachments.
- **Listening & dictation:** device voice, Turkish voice or AI voice; reading speed and pitch; how the dictate microphone works.

- **Permissions:** microphone, camera, notifications, location — status and system settings.
- **Sync:** Google Drive connection and Drive usage, the automatic upload limit, the cache on this device, backups.
- **Account:** sign out, delete account, note lock, **app lock** (asks on launch and when returning from the background; optional "hide in recent apps"); **Full backup** (all notes with attachments, drawings, bubbles and folders in one file), **Restore from backup** (restoring twice doesn't duplicate) and **Import from other apps** (Google Keep Takeout, Markdown/txt, Evernote); privacy policy and licences.

## Tips {#tips}
- The back button first closes an open preview, selection or sheet; only then the note.
- A note you leave empty is deleted when you close it; a note with a reminder is kept.
- Attachments waiting to upload show in the "uploading to the cloud" bar on the home screen; they are safe on this device.
- Put a copied attachment into another note with **+ Add → Paste copied attachment**.
- Hidden gestures are introduced **once**, with a single line, the first time they become possible (list note, first PDF, first image, folders in the grid); at most one tip per session. To see them again: Settings → Modules & quick note → **Show tips again**.
- **Writing with a pen:** touching the note page with a pen writes directly on it and opens the tools (left on tablets, bottom on phones). Tap with a finger to keep typing in the same note; ✓ closes the tools. **Add → Write on page (pen)** opens the same mode. To stop it, turn off **Detect pen automatically** in the pen settings. Handwriting doesn't move if text reflows later.
- **Full screen (tablet, web):** the button in the note's top bar hides the rail and list.
- This guide is always available from the ⋮ menu and from Settings.
