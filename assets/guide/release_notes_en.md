# Release notes

What changed in each update, newest first. The version number is at the bottom of Settings; tap it to check for updates.

## 27 November 2026 {#2026-11-27}
- **New languages: Arabic, Indonesian and Malay.** Pick one in Settings → Appearance → Language (every text in the app is translated). Dictation now listens in the chosen language or the phone's language; before, every language other than Turkish opened the English recogniser. The voice picker lets you choose a voice for each of the five languages.
- **"View & filter" redesigned:** it now looks like the note menu (cards and tiles), and dragging the handle at the top down closes it.
- **Turkish voice (Antalia) removed:** reading aloud uses the phone's own voices; the app is ~28 MB smaller and a previously downloaded voice model (~50 MB) is deleted from the phone automatically.
- **Smarter spell check:** words with a missing or swapped letter such as "lütfn", "önmli", "hseap" now get the right suggestion (there was none before), and correct words like "çoğalt", "çevrilir", "zekâyı" are no longer flagged. Turkish rules no longer kick in while you write Arabic, Indonesian or Malay.
- **Tidier settings:** long descriptions are shorter, "Notes & folders" is split into labelled groups (Gestures, Calendar, Sorting, Shared, Attachments) and language has its own card; truncated button labels were fixed.
- **Smart lists & templates v2 — every list gets its own interactive workspace:** alongside Shopping Store Mode, habits now feature tap-to-toggle 7-day circles, streaks & 30-day heatmaps; moving has timeline vs room/box planners and fragile flags; packing has cabin/checked/wear tabs and a trip wizard; workout has set circles & a built-in rest countdown timer; expenses auto-sum paid and remaining totals; events include a guest-count wizard; gifts track 4 stages; watchlists rate 1–5 stars; and cleaning resets completed cycles.
- **16 smart list kinds with rich bilingual catalogs:** expanded Turkish & English dictionaries and searchable catalogs with one-tap starter packs for shopping, packing, pharmacy, stationery, hardware, camping, baby, sport, moving, cleaning, habits, workout, gifts, watchlist, event, and expense lists.
- **Upgraded Quick List composer:** visual kind switcher, catalog & wizard pills, color-coded group dots, tap-to-edit item sheet, and an option to jump straight into the workspace.
- **Quick Add v2:** natural English prepositions (`add/put/save … to/in/on/into`), arrow routing (`item -> list`), typo-tolerant title matching, instant smart list creation (`yeni bavul listesi: …` / `new packing list: …`), `#tag` & `!urgent` modifiers, inline price extraction, auto-routing across all 16 list kinds, and a **Keep open after adding** toggle for rapid multi-entry.
- **Item menu labels are no longer cut off:** the long-press item menu is now a 4-column grid, so labels like "Move up", "Move down", "Make task" read in full instead of being truncated.
- **Correct spelling in the selection menu:** when you long-press and select an underlined word, the menu (Copy, Select all…) now starts with the correct spelling; tap it to fix the word.
- **Spell check settings no longer hide under the phone's buttons:** the settings opened by long-pressing the spell-fix button now scroll when they don't fit and end above the navigation buttons; **Off** turns checking (including underlines) off completely.
- **Ctrl + mouse wheel changes text size on the web:** holding Ctrl and scrolling up makes text larger, scrolling down makes it smaller (same setting as Settings → Appearance → Text size).
- **The top quick menu is back on the web:** in wide windows the quick menu buttons (pen, spelling, bold…) didn't fit and all fell into "•••", which also overlapped the date; they now show next to the time again.
- **Drive connection on the web lasts longer:** the Drive permission is now kept in the browser, not just the tab, so opening a new tab or reloading no longer asks you to reconnect (Google's permission lasts about an hour; after that one tap renews it). An attachment that failed to download is now retried automatically.
- **Web dictation no longer stops silently:** when the browser's recognizer gives up, the dictation card now closes instead of staying grey as if still listening; long thinking pauses after you've started speaking no longer end listening early.
- **Memory & responsiveness polish for entry-level devices:** adapted image cache limits to device RAM tier (`low`/`mid`/`high`), trimmed oversized image and PDF decoding overhead (`ui.ImageDescriptor` bounds and selective `decodeCommonImage` decoders), removed unused dead code, and compressed bundled wallpapers and non-RFN fonts to shrink the APK.
- **Encrypted notes (E2EE), Play billing & account-deletion hardening:** prevented empty-note cleanup from removing locked/E2EE notes, fixed lock-change/unlock edge cases, tightened Play AI consent/entitlement checks and Cloudflare `/v1/users/me` deletion, and aligned privacy/help docs with shipped features.
- **Shopping, spell check & dictation fixes:** fixed starter shopping aisle IDs and quantity increments, improved Store Mode/receipt/barcode reliability, and refined Turkish spell-check and dictation punctuation handling.

## 26 November 2026 {#2026-11-26}
- **Quick add acts as soon as you say "add":** ending your sentence with "… ekle" (add) now stops listening after ~0.6 s of silence and adds the note after a short ~0.7 s countdown, instead of waiting for silence plus a 2 s countdown. If you don't name a note ("süt ekle") it goes to the selected note; if you name one that can't be found it still asks. You can tap **Wait** during the countdown or undo afterwards.
- **Dictating into a list note creates items:** in a checklist or numbered list, dictating without tapping an item now writes a new item at the end of the list (it used to create plain text above the list). Saying "new item" moves to the next item; no automatic full stop is added at the end of an item.
- **Cleaner dictated text:** on Android 13+, if your phone's recognizer supports it, commas, full stops, question marks and capitals are added automatically. The app also drops fillers like "ııı", "eee", "hmm", adds a question mark to sentences ending with a Turkish question particle, capitalises sentence starts and fixes spacing around punctuation (while smart dictation is on in Settings).
- **A large Turkish dictionary for spell check:** about 27 thousand roots from the open-source Zemberek lexicon, with inflected forms recognised by the suffix analyser (recognition of the 20k most common words: 57% → 89%). Correct words are far less often "corrected" into wrong ones (e.g. "böbrek → börek", "uşak → uçak") and needless underlines are mostly gone; rule-based fixes like "herşey → her şey" still apply. Works offline; the licence is under Settings → Licenses.

- **Redesigned spell check that learns manual fixes:** the tap suggestion card and **Fix by hand** dialog were redesigned from scratch (error/hint badge, wavy preview, crisp suggestion chips, and icon actions). Words you correct via **Fix by hand** or directly in the text are learned in your personal spell memory and suggested next time.
- **Backgrounds now apply to Calendar + 8 new wallpapers (18 total):** your chosen wallpaper, colour, or pattern now flows edge-to-edge behind the status bar on Calendar and Day screens too, with **Background** directly in Calendar's ⋮ menu. Added 8 new wallpapers (Sakura breeze, Ocean mist, Autumn maple, Sage linen, Peach horizon, Nordic paper, Starry pine, Obsidian gold).
- **Inbox ("Gelen") folder and logic fixes:** your own notes no longer trigger phantom unread badges; shared notes no longer flash the empty state while still loading; you can now search inside Inbox and tap **Mark all read**; accepted shopping notes preserve aisle/quantity metadata; custom Inbox names and icons now show consistently in previews and folder swipe stops.
- **End-to-end encrypted locked notes (E2EE), 3 UI profiles & platform polish:** locked note bodies and items are now encrypted client-side with AES-256-GCM before sync; **Settings → Personalise** adds one-tap **Simple / Standard / Tablet & pen** profiles; Android widgets are focused on 3 essentials (**Today**, **Quick add**, **Smart list**); pinning a PDF in tablet split view automatically collapses the left list; fresh empty accounts start with 2 interactive starter notes; memory usage and settings sync are lighter.
- **Home screen background now fills the whole screen:** your background image reaches behind the status bar and the navigation buttons, and flows seamlessly around the search bar. Previously the image didn't fit the screen and left plain strips at the top and bottom; fixed. The note list also scrolls all the way to the bottom.
- **Spell check underlines misspelled words (like Word):** in "Show only" mode, typos with no suggestion of our own (like "petnr", "nabr") now get a red wavy underline too; your phone's spell checker (Gboard) supplies suggestions in the tap menu. Words you added to the dictionary or use often in your notes aren't underlined.
- **Keyboard stops auto-correcting when auto-correct is off:** with spelling set to "Off" or "Show only", the app no longer asks the keyboard to auto-correct; only "Auto" turns it on. If your keyboard still corrects, you may need to turn off "Auto-correction" in its own settings.

## 23 November 2026 {#2026-11-23}
- **Insert a table:** a new **Table** option in the **Add** sheet and in the ⋯ menus of the bottom bar and quick menu: it starts with 3 rows × 2 columns on the line you're typing on, and its first cell opens for typing. Every cell has its own text: tap a cell and type; the keyboard's **Next** moves to the next cell and opens a new row at the end; add as many rows as you like with **+ Add row**. In ⚙ **Table settings**, change the row and column counts instantly with −/+ (it asks first if text would be deleted), make the first row a header, add a row below, delete a row or even out the columns. While typing in a cell, drag the handles between columns to set their widths. Like images, a table can be **Pinned** (at the top, open while you type), **Floating** (on the page; long-press to move, drag the corner to resize) or **Attached** (in the strip; tap to open and edit it full screen). Share it as an **Image**, **PDF** or **Word** file; it's also included in the note's PDF and Word exports (a real, editable table in Word). Typing in cells doesn't rebuild the editor.
- **Insert today's date:** writes today's date as **22/11/2026** with one tap wherever the cursor is (body, list item or table cell). It's in the ⋯ menus of the quick menu and bottom bar; pin it to the quick menu from **Edit quick menu** if you like.
- **Quick menu ⋯ now matches the bottom bar:** while typing, the ⋯ of the quick menu at the top no longer shows just "edit": every button that isn't on the row (Insert today's date, Table, Bullet, Italic, Add photo, Reminder…) appears in the same two-column tiles as the bottom bar's ⋯, with **Edit quick menu** at the bottom.
- **Consistent buttons in split and pinned-media views:** with YouTube, PDF, slides, or images pinned, **Pin**, **Find in note**, star, and urgent indicators now stay in their usual top-bar spot; the pinned pane's ⋮ menu always shows the labelled view switcher and direct **Top / Left / Right** placement choices. In desktop/web split view, **Filter** stays visible in the list header and right-clicking a card offers **Open to the side** and **Note menu**.
- **Smaller app:** 5 core fonts (Inter Tight, Roboto, Lora, Roboto Mono, Caveat) ship with the app; other fonts download once the first time you pick them (cloud icon) and then work offline. Until then, or when offline, the default font is shown.
