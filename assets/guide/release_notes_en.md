# Release notes

What changed in each update, newest first. The version number is at the bottom of Settings; tap it to check for updates.

## 13 November 2026 {#2026-11-13}
- **Voice typing on tablets:** if the device's live speech recognition doesn't work (tablets without Google services), the app notices within a few seconds and switches to the voice note sheet: your speech is recorded and transcribed. Stutter while voice typing is on is also reduced.
- **Smaller floating attachment menu on tablets:** on wide screens the menu under an attachment is a single row under it instead of two full-width rows.
- **Smaller voice recordings on the page:** a floating recording is no longer a long strip on wide screens, just a shorter, lower pill.
- **Active formatting shows in the quick menu:** when the cursor is in bold, italic, underlined, highlighted or colored text, or in a heading, its quick menu button looks pressed.
- **Easier quick menu editing:** ⋮ → "Edit quick menu" now opens a sheet with "In the menu" and "Available" tiles; tap to add or remove, no saving needed, with a live preview on top. The same editor is in Settings → Personalize, so a quick menu that disappeared can be brought back.
- **Back transition on tablets:** leaving a note slides and fades the list in instead of jumping.
- **Erasing no longer changes text size:** after erasing part of a stroke the rest no longer gets thicker or thinner.
- **Writing that crosses pages can be erased:** on PDFs, pen writing that starts on a lower page and runs onto the page above can now be erased there too.
- **Bubble button on the pinned panel:** phones now get the add-bubble button right in the pinned panel's bar.
- **Pen button introduces itself:** the first time a pen is detected the floating pen button blinks a few times.
- **Finger writing notice:** with "pen only" off, a "Finger writes too" notice appears next to the pen button; tap it to switch to pen only.
- **Pen colors on pinned PDFs:** turning the pen on from the panel shows a color dot in the bar that opens every color; on tablets black, blue, red and green sit right in the bar.
- **Eraser is easy to see:** when the eraser is on its button has a filled colored background.
- **Writing no longer vanishes when you tap the eraser:** on pinned or embedded PDFs, handwriting no longer disappears when you tap the eraser (or pen button) and comes back on the next tap.
- **Pinned attachment panel redesigned:** on phones the bar shows only full screen, Take note, pen and ⋮; view (Pinned/Floating/Attachment), bubbles, browse pages and bookmarks for PDFs, share and remove all live in a single ⋮ menu. The bar no longer overflows with large text or on narrow screens.
- **Pen buttons stay in sync:** turning on the floating pen button no longer hides the panel's pen, it shows as on; turning the panel's pen off also turns off the floating button and pen mode. Turning the pen on from the panel shows eraser, undo, redo and pen settings in the bar.
- **Pen hints stay out of the way:** messages like "pen writes / finger touches" appear above the pen panel at the bottom and are shown once per state.
- **Pinned attachment on the side on tablets:** if you never changed the layout, holding a wide screen in landscape opens the pinned attachment on the left and the note on the right; portrait keeps it on top. A layout you pick is remembered.
- **Smoother keyboard with a pinned attachment:** the pinned panel is no longer laid out on every frame while the keyboard opens and closes.
- **Drag to trash inside folders too:** with a folder, filter or search open you can still long-press a note and drag it onto the trash bin to delete it.
- **Make the bottom bar yours:** in Settings → Personalize → "Note screen · bottom bar" drag buttons to reorder them, remove the ones you don't use, and add undo, redo, reminder, photo, camera, scan, color and share.
- **Smoother underlined items:** dotted, dashed and wavy item underlines are drawn with far less work, so opening the keyboard and scrolling should stutter less.
- **Honest screens when offline:** without internet the "Inbox" filter shows "No connection" with Retry instead of "nothing here"; the AI sheet tells you up front that you are offline (buttons turn on when you are back); waiting attachments say "will upload when you are online" instead of "uploading".
- **Text selection on the web fixed:** double-clicking with the mouse now selects just the word (not the whole sentence); shift+arrow selection moves letter by letter instead of jumping to the whole word.
- **Calendar events on every device:** new events now show on all your devices by default ("All devices"); pick Google in the editor to save to Google Calendar instead.
- **Smoother transitions:** opening a note, typing the first letter of a search, swiping between filters/folders and flipping weeks in the calendar now do less work; the writing area no longer jitters while the format panel opens or closes.
- **Lighter Drive previews on cards:** thumbnails are decoded at card size, so scrolling the list uses less memory.

## 12 November 2026 {#2026-11-12}
- **Markdown (.md) files:** add a .md from "Add file" inside a note or by drag and drop; tapping it opens a clean, readable view and you can add all or part of it to the note. A .md shared from another app or opened with "Open with" becomes a new note with its title, items and checkboxes.
- **Faster start:** a splash screen with the app icon instead of a white flash; the app is ready a bit sooner.
- **No needless spinners:** short loads no longer flash a spinner (it appears only after 0.3 s).
- **PDF preview while opening:** a thumbnail of the document instead of a grey box.
- **Simpler pinned-attachment panel:** the "T" button is gone; Plain text and hide/show bubbles live in one bubble menu.
- **Side pinning on tablets:** a pinned attachment can sit left/right in non-fullscreen windows too (list + note side by side).
- **No jumping when dragging to the bin:** reordering freezes as you approach the bin.
- **Web:** item gestures are off by default (they broke text selection); the caret uses the text colour so it stays visible on coloured notes.
- **More readable dark theme:** cards stand out a bit more from the background in dark mode, PDF previews get a soft dimming layer, and the Delete row is clearer.
- **Calendar strip fits with large text:** with a large system font, day names shrink so the strip no longer overflows.
- **Nicer on the computer (web):** the focused item shows a clear ring when you move with the keyboard; section lines on wide screens run to the end of the grid.
- **Note bottom bar refreshed:** Pen now sits in the left capsule (Format · Add · Pen · Find · Attachments); Share moved to the note menu (⋮). On very narrow screens Pen leaves the bar and stays in the quick row while typing.
- **Easier taps on the bar:** every bottom bar button has at least a 48 dp touch area.
- **Bar follows the note colour:** in coloured notes the capsules, icons and active button take the note's tone.
- **Shorter note menu:** no drag handle; Edit menu/screen and Settings are under ⚙; Delete sits at the bottom in red. On small phones Delete needs almost no scrolling.
- **Sheets look alike:** AI and Share sheets use the same tiles; sheet titles are one size; delete confirmations have a red button.
- **Empty and offline screens:** empty lists say "No … here" in one pattern; without a connection you see "No connection", on failure "Couldn't load" (Inbox no longer looks empty on errors).
- **Folders, Trash, Archive, Settings:** the empty folder card is smaller and the "New folder" tile left the grid; Trash and Archive have Search and ⋮ on top and labelled "Restore" and "Delete" on cards; Settings has plain icon boxes and a guest card; agenda rows share one size.
- **Calmer home screen:** the home button and microphone no longer stand out; "+" is the only filled button.
- **Day abbreviations in the calendar strip:** days show short names instead of a single letter.
- **Completed items in one line on cards:** checked items collapse to "✓ 3 completed"; to see them as before, Settings → Appearance → Shown on cards → "Completed items".
- **One button on an empty home screen:** with no notes yet, "Write your first note".
- **Calmer screens:** headings and labels are slightly lighter, and very small text was raised to a readable size.
- **Colours carry meaning:** the theme's main colour is for actions, the warm colour for time and reminders, the third colour only for AI (✨). The "Due" strip is now warm.
- **Plain icon circles:** the Copy/PDF/Image circles in Share and the icon tiles in Settings are neutral.
- **Theme picker in three groups:** Calm, Paper and Vivid.
- **No list stutter while typing on tablets:** in side-by-side view the list no longer rebuilds on every save while you type; the card updates a few seconds after you stop. Switching from the open note to another one is lighter too.
- **More writing space on landscape tablets:** in a short window with the keyboard open, the bottom bar hides, the top bar gets thinner and a pinned attachment shrinks to its smallest size.
- **Simpler narrow list pane:** when the list pane is narrow, the calendar and due strips hide and the AI and filter buttons move into the ⋮ menu.
- **List pane on the right:** on tablets, long-press the divider or use Settings → Appearance to move the note list to the right.
- **New trash can:** just drag a note onto the trash and let go, no waiting. The note shrinks and is pulled in as it gets closer, the trash turns red, and on release the note spins into it. "Undo" brings it back. The trash icon no longer stays on screen after dropping.
- **Two-step hold on items:** holding an item shows a filling ring; at the first light vibration all of the item's text is selected, keep holding and at the second vibration item selection starts.
- **Checked items stay checked on cards:** tapping a checked item's box on a home screen card no longer unchecks it; the note opens and you can uncheck it there.
- **Calendar swipe can be turned off:** Settings → Notes lets you turn off swiping between weeks/days in the calendar. To stop swiping between folders on the home screen, set "Swiping left/right on the home screen" to "Off" in the same place.
- **Write on pinned images:** you can write with the pen directly on a pinned image; the ink scales with the image when you zoom.
- **Pen button in full screen:** PDF, image and slide full screen views have a "pen writes / touches" button; the pen writes without pressing the draw button, the finger pans.
- **Finger writing hint on phones:** turning on pen mode with a finger shows a one-time hint: one finger draws, two fingers scroll.
- **Eraser and tray in PDFs:** the eraser works on attached PDFs too; the tray's colour and width apply when writing on PDFs; undo removes the last stroke where you wrote last.
- **Pending attachment reminder:** if attachments are still waiting to upload when you close the app, a quiet reminder comes later; tap a pending attachment for "Try now".
- **PDF cards everywhere:** cards of notes with PDFs show the first page on the web and other devices too.
- **Shorter attachment hint:** the hint after adding an attachment is one line and disappears in a few seconds.
- **"📖 s.N" line is editable:** with a PDF in "Attachment" view, tapping the leftover page line in the note lets you edit or delete it.

## 11 November 2026 {#2026-11-11}
- **No more jitter when the keyboard opens:** the note screen is no longer rebuilt on every keyboard frame; the stutter when you start typing right after opening a long list is gone too.
- **Bottom bar while typing:** Settings → Cards lets you turn off hiding the bottom bar while typing, and set how long until it hides and reappears in 0.1-second steps.
- **Hold to select an item:** a long press on an item selects its text; keep holding a little longer and, with a light vibration, item selection starts.
- **Drag to delete:** on the home screen, while dragging a note a trash can appears at the bottom; let go once the ring fills and the note moves to the trash (Undo brings it back).
- **Hidden attachments are mentioned:** when a card hides its images, a short line like "YouTube video · 2 images" says what is attached.
- **Customize the Add menu:** use the settings icon in the + panel to hide, show and reorder options by dragging; "Reset to default" restores it.
- **New fountain pen:** the pen tray has a fountain pen that gets thicker as you press and thinner when you write fast. Drawing is smoother: the rest of the page is no longer repainted while you draw.
- **More secure:** reminder notifications for locked notes no longer show the title or content; friend requests and sharing are now more secure.
- **Auto-correct rebuilt:** it only fixes words it is sure about; if you change a correction or press Backspace right after it, that fix is never made again. Settings → Learned spellings adds a "Rules only" mode and a list of rejected fixes you can restore.
- **Handwriting survives backups:** full backups and shared notes now keep the handwriting you drew on the page; previously it was lost when a note was restored or shared.
- **Large PDFs can be added:** PDFs over the cloud limit (e.g. 1 GB) are no longer refused; they're copied into the app without loading into memory, kept only on this device and not backed up to the cloud.

## 10 November 2026 {#2026-11-10}
- **Reopen-after-update permission:** Notes now appears in the "Display over other apps" list. Tap "Reopen after updating: allow" at the bottom of Settings and turn it on; the app then opens by itself when an update finishes.
- **What's new on Home:** after an update, a short notice on Home shows what changed in this version; "See all" opens the release notes.
- **Updates visible in Settings:** tapping the version line now shows download percentage and a bar, then "installing"; cancellation or errors are shown there too.
- **The app reopens after updating itself** instead of just closing. If the phone blocks opening from the background, a "Notes updated — tap to open" notification appears.
- **Voice typing:** fixed a sentence being written a second time (sometimes cut off) after a pause; when the recognizer re-transcribes the same sentence only one copy is kept.
- **Updates rewritten:** when a new version is out, a persistent "New version available" strip appears at the top of Home; tap it to download (with progress) and install. With automatic updates on, the new version downloads in the background and installs the next time you open the app. On Android 12+, after the app has updated itself once, there's no confirmation dialog at all. You can turn automatic updates off at the bottom of Settings.
- The **View & filter** sheet is more compact: the empty space at the top is gone and on/off options are small check chips. Showing/hiding the **folder row** and **calendar strip** is here now (moved from the ⋮ menu).
- Pinned PDFs now scroll continuously: all pages one under another, no page-by-page turning or sideways swipe. The page number sits small at the bottom right; tap it to jump to a page.
- With the eraser selected, it now erases on PDFs attached to a note too (it used to draw). The colour and width picked in the pen tray also apply when writing on a PDF.
- Undo removes the last stroke where you last wrote (page or PDF).
- Home: tapping **All** shows every note (folder and filters reset); long-press All for the Folders screen. Only one chip is highlighted at a time.
- An open list package no longer closes when you tap Format or the bottom bar; packing/unpacking no longer rebuilds the whole list.
- In a guest account the note screen now says "Only on this device" instead of "Synced".
- Writing with the pen on a pinned or in-page PDF no longer stutters on every stroke (each stroke used to rebuild and save the whole note).
- Writing with the pen on pinned and in-page PDFs works again (pen mode opened but left no ink); same fix for note packages.
- On a device that has seen a pen, the finger scrolls instead of drawing in pen mode; a resting palm no longer scrolls the page.
- Pinned PDF fullscreen opens on the current page; pages and ink from fullscreen carry back.
- Fixed stutter and flicker while the keyboard opens/closes and while typing.
- Less stutter when closing a note on a portrait tablet.
- Web: the × that deletes an item works.
- Settings → **Release notes**: every update, item by item.
- Settings → **Pen, writing and screen**: settings that were only reachable from the note screen (pen, spell fix, home strips, holidays, attachment strip, focus mode) in one place.
